# Code of Conduct

FolioWeave is a small open-source project. Everyone who takes part in it, in
issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, is expected to follow this.

## Expected

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not with the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Keep feedback about the work: specific, useful and kind.
- Help keep the project open to people of every background and level of
  experience.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or unwelcome advances.

## Reporting

Tell the maintainer privately, through the contact details on
[kukutx.vercel.app](https://kukutx.vercel.app). Say what happened and where;
links help. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that break these rules, and may block someone from the project
for a time or for good. Decisions are made case by case and explained to the
people involved.
