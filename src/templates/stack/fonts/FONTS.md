# Stack's fonts

Self-hosted WOFF2 files, split into Latin and Latin Extended as Google Fonts
serves them (see `../fonts.ts`). All three families are under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1; the licence and their copyright lines are in `OFL.txt`.

| File                          | Family           | Axes kept                       |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------- |
| `instrument-serif-*.woff2`    | Instrument Serif | regular and italic              |
| `source-serif-latin*.woff2`   | Source Serif 4   | weight 400–600, optical size 16 |
| `source-serif-italic-*.woff2` | Source Serif 4   | italic 400, optical size 16     |
| `geist-mono.woff2`            | Geist Mono       | weight 100–900, Latin           |
